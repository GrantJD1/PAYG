How the fare is worked out
  1. Find the pay as you go fare for the journey. A pay as you go return is always the equivalent of 2x pay as you go singles.
  2. Find the equivalent non-pay as you go fare for the same journey and ticket type (single or return).
  3. Take the Railcard discount off the equivalent non-pay as you go fare: 1/3 off for most Railcards, 50% off for the 16-17 Saver. Railcard fares are rounded down to the nearest 5p, and 1/3 off Railcards take 33.4% off.
  4. You pay whichever is cheaper: the pay as you go fare, or the equivalent non-pay as you go fare with the Railcard discount. For a return, if two Railcard singles would be cheaper still, you pay that.
  5. The Railcard discount is never taken off the pay as you go fare itself.
